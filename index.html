How Scoring Works

Read this before you pick — no surprises later!

1. Pick 8 golfers across 5 tiers: Tier 1 (1 pick), Tier 2 (2 picks), Tier 3 (2 picks), Tier 4 (2 picks), Tier 5 (1 pick). Tiers are based on betting odds — Tier 1 has the favorites, Tier 5 the longshots.

2. Points are based on finishing position: Each golfer earns points based on where they finish in the actual PGA Championship. Lower points = better. A win is 1 point, 2nd place is 2 points, and so on up the scale.

3. Best 4 of 8 count: Only your 4 lowest-scoring (best-finishing) golfers count toward your total. This gives you some insurance if a pick misses the cut or has a rough weekend.

4. Lowest total wins: Add up the points from your best 4 golfers. The person with the lowest combined score wins the pool.

5. Tiebreakers (if needed): If two or more people have the same total points, ties are broken by: (1) closest prediction of the winner's final score vs. par, (2) correct playoff prediction, (3) correct Saturday moving day leader.

6. Deadline: All picks must be submitted by Thursday, May 14 at 11:59 PM ET (midnight tonight). You can edit your picks as many times as you want before the deadline using your name and passcode. After the deadline, all picks are locked and visible to everyone on the leaderboard.

7. Live scoring: The leaderboard updates every 5 minutes during tournament play using live PGA Tour data. Scores will fluctuate throughout each round as golfers move up and down the leaderboard. Final standings are determined after the tournament concludes Sunday evening.

Good luck, and may the best picks win! 🏅
